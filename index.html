08 / GOOD TO KNOW

QUESTIONS.
ANSWERED.

New to EXILERS HQ? Here's a quick guide to joining in, finding events, and getting involved.

JOIN THE COMMUNITY ↗

EXILERS HQ is a gaming community concept focused on connecting players, hosting events, and creating competitive and social gaming experiences. Join the official community for the current games and activities.

Use the official EXILERS HQ Discord invitation linked in the Join section. Follow the server's welcome instructions and community rules once you arrive.

Check official announcements for event dates, eligibility, team sizes, registration instructions, rules, and any prize information. Only treat an event as confirmed when it has been announced by the EXILERS HQ team.

Entry requirements can vary by event. Check the official event announcement for any fees, eligibility requirements, prize terms, and registration deadlines.

Staff recruitment depends on community needs. Watch the official announcements and follow any application process shared by the EXILERS HQ management team.

Confirmed scores and standings should be published through official event announcements. The scoreboard on this website is a visual showcase until real match data is added.
